# How long do AI jobs stay open in Europe? (November 2026 data)

Period: 2026-10-15 to 2026-11-11. Published 2026-11-11.

Days listed for 319 remote and hybrid AI roles that closed between 15 October and 11 November 2026, from a daily scrape of employer applicant-tracking systems across Europe. Days listed runs from the first to the last day the employer's own board showed the role.

Full report: https://remotelyaijobs.com/insights/ai-job-market-reports/how-long-ai-jobs-stay-open-europe/

Files: `facts.json` (everything), plus CSVs for each table.
